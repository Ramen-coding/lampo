⚡ Lampo

Your Lightning node,
in your pocket.

A small remote for your own LND node, over Lightning Node Connect. No open ports, no Tor, no custodian.

1 · Pair once

Scan the session QR.

Create an LNC session in Lightning Terminal, then scan its QR or paste the 10‑word phrase.

2 · Balance

Your balance,
at a glance.

Tap it: sats → euros → hidden. Hidden stays hidden, even after a restart.

2 · Balance

Pull to refresh.

If the node is slow to answer, Lampo reconnects on its own.

3 · Your QR

Your QR,
one tap away.

Keep the QR of your Lightning Address in the app, ready to show.

4 · Pay

Scan and pay.

Tap Invia, then scan or paste an invoice, a Lightning Address or an LNURL. Check the amount, then pay.

4 · Pay

Paid.

The routing fee is shown after every payment.

5 · Receive

Type an amount,
show the QR.

Lampo creates the invoice on your node and waits for the payment.

5 · Receive

Received.

You see it the moment the invoice is settled.

6 · Locked

Opens with
Face ID.

Every time you come back: Face ID, fingerprint or your phone passcode. Keys stay in the Keychain / Android encrypted storage.
